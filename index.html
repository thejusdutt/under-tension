Chest

1

DB Bench Press

DEEP · ELBOWS BELOW TORSO · BENCH OR STEP

Lie on a bench or step so the elbows can travel below torso level — the floor cuts the range short. Lower 2–4 s, pause deep, press up.

2–4 SETS · 5–30 REPS · 0–2 RIR

--:--
2

Deficit Push-Up

HANDS ON DB HANDLES · EXTRA DEPTH

Grip the dumbbell handles as a deficit so the chest can sink below hand level. Pause in the bottom stretch; take sets close to failure.

2–4 SETS · 5–30 REPS · 0–2 RIR

--:--
3

DB Flye

STRETCH-BIASED · LIGHT · CONTROLLED

Light load, soft elbows. Lower wide until the pecs pull, hold the stretch, sweep back up. Control is the exercise — don't load it heavy.

2–3 SETS · 10–30 REPS · 0–2 RIR

02/06

Shoulders

1

DB Lateral Raise

SIDE DELTS · NO COMPOUND SUBSTITUTE

No compound movement trains the side delts — this is irreplaceable. Raise to horizontal, lower slower than you lifted, no swing.

3–4 SETS · 10–30 REPS · 0–2 RIR

--:--
2

Seated DB Overhead Press

FULL ROM · FRONT DELTS + TRICEPS

Press from shoulder height to lockout and let the DBs come all the way back down every rep — the bottom is where the front delts work longest.

2–4 SETS · 5–20 REPS · 0–2 RIR

--:--
3

Bent-Over DB Reverse Flye

REAR DELTS · MOST UNDER-TRAINED HEAD

Hinge over, let the arms hang, sweep back and up with soft elbows. The rear delt is the most under-trained head — don't let this slot slide.

2–4 SETS · 10–30 REPS · 0–2 RIR

03/06

Back

1

One-Arm DB Row

FULL PROTRACTION AT THE BOTTOM

Let the shoulder blade fully protract at the bottom — the DB should pull you into a stretch — then row to the hip and squeeze.

2–4 SETS · 5–20 REPS · 0–2 RIR

--:--
2

Chin-Up / Inverted Row

HEAVIEST HOME LOADING AVAILABLE

The heaviest back loading you can get at home. Dead hang at the bottom, chin past the bar. No bar? Inverted rows under a sturdy table.

2–4 SETS · TO 0–2 RIR

--:--
3

DB Pullover

LONG-LENGTH LAT LOADING

One DB, both hands. Lower it in an arc behind the head until the lats stretch hard, hold, then pull back over the chest with near-straight arms.

2–3 SETS · 8–20 REPS · 0–2 RIR

04/06

Biceps

1

Incline DB Curl

ARM BEHIND TORSO · LONG-HEAD STRETCH

Recline so the arm hangs behind the torso — that stretches the long head. Curl without letting the elbow drift forward; pause at the hang.

2–4 SETS · 8–20 REPS · 0–2 RIR

--:--
2

Standing Supinating DB Curl

ROTATE PALM-UP THROUGH THE CURL

Start neutral at the bottom, rotate the palm up as you curl, reverse the rotation slowly on the way down.

2–4 SETS · 8–20 REPS · 0–2 RIR

--:--
3

Hammer Curl

BRACHIALIS + BRACHIORADIALIS

Neutral grip the whole rep. Trains brachialis and brachioradialis — arm thickness the supinated curls don't cover.

2–3 SETS · 8–20 REPS · 0–2 RIR

--:--
05/06

Triceps

1

Overhead DB Extension

STRONGEST PICK IN THE LITERATURE

Both hands on one DB overhead. Lower behind the head to a deep long-head stretch, hold, extend. The strongest exercise-selection finding in the literature.

2–4 SETS · 8–20 REPS · 0–2 RIR

--:--
2

Lying DB Extension

SKULLCRUSHER · SWEEP PAST THE HEAD

Sweep the DBs back past the head, not down to the forehead — the arc keeps the long head loaded at length. Elbows stay pointed up.

2–4 SETS · 8–20 REPS · 0–2 RIR

--:--
3

Diamond Push-Up

HANDS TOGETHER · ELBOWS TRACK BACK

Hands together under the chest, elbows tracking back. Push-ups matched for load grow the triceps like pressing — take these near failure.

2–4 SETS · TO 0–2 RIR

--:--
06/06

Legs

1

DB Bulgarian Split Squat

UNILATERAL · DOUBLES EFFECTIVE LOAD

Rear foot elevated. One working leg doubles what 30 kg is worth. Sink until the front glute and rear hip flexor stretch, drive up through the whole foot.

2–4 SETS / LEG · 5–20 REPS · 0–2 RIR

--:--
2

DB Romanian Deadlift

SEMIMEMBRANOSUS + GLUTES

Soft knees, hips travel back, spine long. Lower until the hamstrings stop you — not the floor — hold the stretch, drive the hips through.

2–4 SETS · 5–20 REPS · 0–2 RIR

--:--
3

Nordic Hamstring Curl

SEMITENDINOSUS · PAIRS WITH THE RDL

Anchor the ankles, lower the torso as slowly as you can, push back up off the hands. Grows a different hamstring head than the RDL — run both.

2–3 SETS · 3–8 REPS · 0–2 RIR

Evidence

  1. Surface EMG "activation" is not a validated predictor of hypertrophy (Vigotsky et al., Sports Medicine 2022) — these picks come from longitudinal training trials, not EMG.
  2. Hypertrophy is load-independent when sets are taken near failure (López et al., MSSE 2020).
  3. Overhead triceps extension produced ~1.4–1.5× more growth than the neutral-arm position over 12 weeks (Maeo et al., 2022).
  4. Push-ups matched for load produced comparable pec/triceps growth to bench press (Kikuchi & Nakazato, 2017).
  5. Nordic curl grows semitendinosus, stiff-leg deadlift grows semimembranosus — different heads, so run both (Morin et al., J Appl Physiol 2025).
